Entry for the [Six Words, One Story](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deranalabs-six-words-one-story-f982e410) bounty on make.fun — one complete story in exactly six words.

## The story

> She waited fifty years. He came.

Six words, one lifetime. She waited; after fifty years, he came — or came too late. The ending leaves the reader to fill in why she waited, and what happens next.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network) writes the story to `/output/entry.txt`. The file is exactly six words and 32 characters, inside the gate's 1–60 character limit.

```sh
python3 main.py            # writes /output/entry.txt
python3 main.py /tmp/out   # or any output directory
```
